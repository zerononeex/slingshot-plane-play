Slingshot Plane — asset credits
================================

3D MODELS
---------
None yet. The plane, trees, rocks, clouds, coins, flags and slingshot are all
built in code from three.js primitives (see site/js/plane.js and site/js/world.js).

The build sandbox could not reach Kenney (kenney.nl), Quaternius (quaternius.com)
or Poly Pizza (poly.pizza), and no CC0 aeroplane model was found in the mirrors it
could reach, so no GLB model ships in v0. When a CC0 plane model is added, list it
here like this:

  plane.glb — "<model name>" by <author>, <source URL>, licence CC0 1.0
